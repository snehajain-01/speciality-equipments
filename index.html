About Speciality Equipments

Company Overview

Speciality Equipments is a leading solution provider for industrial water treatment, serving manufacturing industries across India from Indore since 1995. We supply 80+ products across water treatment, storage and level instrumentation — sourced from India's most reputed manufacturers: Ion Exchange India Limited, Sintex and Pune Techtrol. Our core expertise is water treatment: from raw water clarification and filtration to softening, demineralisation and reverse osmosis systems. We go beyond equipment supply — our team helps clients identify the right solution, select appropriate specifications and ensure seamless integration into their plants.

👤

Leadership

The company is led by Manish Jain, Proprietor, who brings decades of experience in providing solutions for industrial water and waste water treatment problems, and has grown Speciality Equipments to serve 500+ clients across manufacturing industries throughout India.
